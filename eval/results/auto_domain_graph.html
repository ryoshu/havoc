Role Access

Manager Salesperson Finance Receptionist

Manager inherits sales + finance.
Receptionist: customers + test drives only.
Finance: credit + deal approval only.

Deal Lifecycle

  • negotiating → financing → approved → closed
  • any → lost

Offer Lifecycle

  • pending → accepted | rejected | countered | expired

Trade-In Lifecycle

  • pending_appraisal → appraised → accepted → applied
  • appraised → declined

Credit Lifecycle

  • not_started → submitted → approved | denied | conditional

Domain Constraints

  • ✗ Cannot accept offer below invoice price
  • ✗ Near-floor offers require manager
  • ✗ Cannot approve deal without credit approval
  • ✗ Cannot apply unappraised trade-in
  • ✗ Cannot double-book test drive
  • ✗ Cannot create deal on sold vehicle
  • ✗ Receptionist cannot create deals
  • ✗ Finance cannot make offers
